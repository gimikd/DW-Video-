Pixels Per Foot for Facial Recognition
WHAT IS PPF?
Pixels Per Foot (PPF) measures how many pixels cover one linear foot of the scene at a given distance. Higher PPF = more detail = better recognition.
PPF = H. Resolution ÷ Scene Width (ft)
Scene Width = 2 × Distance × tan(HFOV ÷ 2)

FACIAL RECOGNITION SYSTEMS
The DORI "Identify" level (≥ 76 PPF / 250 PPM) is the minimum for FR enrollment and matching. High-confidence forensic-grade FR typically requires 100+ PPF.

TIPS FOR HIGHER PPF
• Use narrower FOV — tightest varifocal setting
• 4K gives 2× the PPF of 1080p at same FOV
• PPF drops linearly with distance — closer = better
• Mount at face height to minimize distortion
• Avoid ultra-wide lenses for FR applications
